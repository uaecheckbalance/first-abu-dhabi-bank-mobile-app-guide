# First Abu Dhabi Bank Mobile App: A Guide for UAE Customers

By Daniel Collins

The First Abu Dhabi Bank mobile app puts everyday banking on your phone. You can check balances, move money, pay bills and manage cards without visiting a branch, as long as you have a secure connection and your login details ready.

## What the app is for

The app is designed for routine banking tasks. It gives a quick view of your accounts, recent transactions and card activity, and it lets you complete common actions in a few steps. It is a practical choice for customers who want to manage money on the go in the UAE.

## Getting started

1. Download the official app from your phone app store.
2. Register with the details requested by the bank, such as your account or card information.
3. Set a strong password and turn on the security options offered by the app.
4. Sign in and review your account list to confirm everything looks correct.

## Everyday features

- Check account and card balances at a glance.
- View recent transactions and download statements where available.
- Transfer money between your own accounts or to other people.
- Pay utility bills and other regular payments.
- Manage cards, including viewing card details and reporting a problem.

For more detail on the mobile banking options, see the [first abu dhabi bank mobile app](https://uaecheckbalance.com/fab-mobile-app/) guide.

## Checking a balance in the app

Open the app, sign in, and choose the account you want to review. The balance shown should match your recent activity. If a payment is still pending, it may appear separately or update after it clears, so give recent transactions a short time to settle.

## If something looks wrong

Check the transaction list first and compare it with your receipts. If a payment is unfamiliar, note the date, amount and merchant name, then contact the bank through the official support channel in the app or on its website.

## Safety tips

- Never share your password, PIN or one-time code with anyone.
- Lock your phone and keep the app updated.
- Avoid signing in on public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.
- Sign out if you use a shared device.
